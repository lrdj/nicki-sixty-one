Prepare

Before you go

Two short forms, once only

The clinic asks first-time clients to fill these in before arriving. If they are not done, they may have to cut the treatment short to complete the paperwork on the day, so it is worth five minutes beforehand. The health questionnaire covers both appointments.

  • Health questionnaire needed before the first visit on 6 November, covers both treatments
  • Kobido questionnaire for the Kobido session only

For the Kobido session Fri 6 Nov

  • Come with a clean face and no heavy make-up, so they can work directly on skin and muscle.
  • Skip heavy creams or oils beforehand, as they affect the flow of the massage.
  • Drink plenty of water during the day.
  • Give yourself a moment to slow down before you arrive, so you can sink into it.
  • Come ready to chat: the first part is a consultation about what you would like from it.

For the Ayurvedic massage Sat 28 Nov

  • There is a quick chat first to get a sense of your dosha and preferences.
  • Expect warm herbal oils and long, flowing strokes from head to toe. Not deep pressure.
  • You will probably be a bit oily afterwards, so wear something comfortable you do not mind.
  • Afterwards, wrap up and stay cosy for the rest of the day to keep the balanced feeling going.
  • Do not plan anything demanding afterwards. Falling asleep on the table is apparently normal.

If plans change

Both bookings can be moved online. The clinic asks for at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel. If you really cannot make it, you are welcome to send someone in your place at no charge.
